## Capability map (leadership)

| Capability | Observable behaviour | Practice activity | Tool shipped | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Set priorities | Turns team goals into weekly priorities and trade-offs | Priority simulation | Weekly priority template | Team pulse item |
| Give feedback | Timely, specific, behaviour-based | Role play on real cases | Feedback script | Team pulse item |
| Coach | Asks before telling; employee owns next step | Coaching practice triads | Question bank | Observation |
| Run 1:1s | Recurring agenda, commitments followed up | 1:1 audit + peer review | 1:1 agenda | 1:1 consistency |
| Handle conflict | Addresses tension early and neutrally | Scenario discussion | Conflict prep sheet | Escalation pattern |

## 1:1 agenda (manager tool)

```text
1. Priorities — top priorities; decisions, context or blockers needed
2. Feedback — what went well; one behaviour or outcome to adjust
3. Growth — skill or responsibility to build next; support needed
4. Follow-up — employee commitments · manager commitments · next date
```
