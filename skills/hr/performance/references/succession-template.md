# Succession and talent review template

Used by `/hr:perform succession`. Save to `plans/hr/<slug>/succession-plan.md` — the role list and
readiness counts only. Named-role + successor rows, individual 9-box placements and per-successor
plans: fill in the HRIS talent module; a pseudonym beside a unique role is still identifying (hr-rules § 3).

## 1. Critical role map

Score each role 1–3 on each factor; criteria agreed with the leadership sponsor before scoring.

| Role | Business impact if vacant | Replaceability (3 = hard) | Vacancy likelihood | Score | Critical? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Head of X] | | | | | |

Start with a focused list a leadership team can review properly; widen in later cycles.

## 2. Potential criteria (written before the session)

Potential is separate from current performance. Example criteria — adapt and define each with
behavioural anchors:
- **Learning agility** — picks up unfamiliar problems quickly; applies lessons from setbacks.
- **Scope appetite and capacity** — seeks and handles larger, more ambiguous responsibility.
- **Leadership behaviours** — develops others, influences without authority, decides with transparency.

Not potential: tenure, visibility to senior leaders, similarity to the current role-holder,
willingness to relocate unless the role truly requires it, any protected characteristic (hr-rules § 5).

## 3. 9-box definitions

|  | Lower performance | Moderate performance | High performance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High potential** | Potential not yet shown in results — diagnose fit and support | Development priority | Core succession pool |
| **Moderate potential** | Address performance | Solid contributor — develop in role | Strong performer — stretch within scope |
| **Lower potential** | Performance plan / role fit review | Steady contributor | Valued expert — retain, not pipeline |

A high performer with no interest in larger scope is not "low value" — they are a different investment.

## 4. Session plan

```text
Pre-work (one week before):
- Each leader submits performance (recent cycles) and potential ratings, separately, with evidence
- HR compiles a single grid; flags disagreements and unsupported placements
- Facilitation guide with the criteria in section 2 shared in advance
Session:
1. Restate definitions; remind: potential ≠ performance
2. Quadrant by quadrant (start with the core pool); sponsor gives one specific example per placement
3. Challenges require behavioural evidence; strong disagreement → follow-up, not a vote
4. Map candidates to critical roles with readiness tiers
5. Assign a development owner and a conversation date to each named successor
After:
- Group distribution check on placements and nominations (aggregate, hr-rules § 5)
- Development conversations sequenced by sensitivity
```

## 5. Coverage table

Fill the per-successor rows (successor, readiness, gaps, retention risk) in the HRIS talent module;
`plans/` holds the role list and readiness counts:

| Critical role (family / band) | Ready now (n) | 1–2 yrs (n) | 3+ yrs (n) | No successor? | Gap themes | Development owner (role) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Role family, band] | | | | yes / no | | |

Readiness: **Ready now** (could step in with minimal support) · **1–2 years** (trajectory, needs
targeted development) · **3+ years** (early-stage) · **No successor** (critical gap — internal
acceleration or external bench).

Coverage summary: roles with more than one successor [ ] · single successor [ ] · none [ ].

## 6. Successor development plan (per candidate) — fill in the HRIS talent module, not `plans/`

```markdown
Target role: [ ]   Readiness now: [ ]   Target readiness: [ ] by [date]
Gaps from calibration: [1] [2] [3]
| Gap | Experience / stretch assignment | Relationship support (mentor, sponsor) | Formal learning | Milestone | Owner | Date |
Retention check: role and scope · recognition and pay (→ hr-rewards) · direction · manager relationship
Conversation: date [ ] — what they are told about pipeline status per policy; confidentiality of the list kept
```

A stretch assignment closes a named gap and has a sponsor and a review date — extra workload without
developmental intent is not development.

## 7. Flight-risk diagnosis (before assuming a departure)

| Dimension | Questions |
|---|---|
| Role and scope | Expected mandate missing? New leaders hired above them? |
| Recognition and pay | Reviewed recently against market? Pay as proxy for feeling undervalued? (→ hr-rewards) |
| Direction and culture | Do they see their place in the next chapter? |
| Manager relationship | Changed recently? Same pattern in the team? |
| The conversation itself | Has anyone held a structured stay conversation, or is this hearsay? |

## 8. Post-transition stabilisation

| Checkpoint | Question | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 30 days | Is the successor using the role's decision authority, or deferring? Are direct reports routing around them? | HRBP |
| 60 days | Are peer and cross-functional relationships working? Are calibration gaps showing, and is support in place? | HRBP + sponsor |
| 90 days | Would the successor, team and peers call the transition complete? What structural or development adjustments are needed? | HRBP |

## 9. Leadership / board summary

```markdown
- Coverage: [n] critical roles — [n] with more than one successor, [n] single, [n] none
- Most significant gap and mitigation: [ ] — owner [ ] — closing date [ ]
- Active development actions: [n] successors with plans; [n] stretch assignments in progress
- Retention risks being managed: [count, no names]
- Ask: [budget / sponsor time / decision]
- Next review: [date]
```

Frame gaps as known, managed risks with dates — neither "solved" nor "hopeless". No external coverage
or readiness benchmark without a cited source — `[NEEDS DATA]`.
