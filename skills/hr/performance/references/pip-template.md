# Performance improvement plan (PIP) template

Used by `/hr:perform pip`. Save to `plans/hr/<slug>/pip.md` with a pseudonym (`Employee A`). The named,
signed version is produced and stored in the HRIS — never in `plans/hr/` (hr-rules § 3).

Run the fitness test in SKILL.md § PIP first. If expectations were never set, feedback was never
given, the cause is structural, or leave/health/adjustment may be involved → stop; this template is
not the next step.

## 1. Pre-draft checklist

- [ ] Jurisdiction named; local rules on warnings, plan periods, representation and dismissal checked `[VERIFY: <country> law + collective agreement]`
- [ ] Current role expectations / job description attached by reference
- [ ] Each concern has dates, examples and business impact
- [ ] Prior feedback conversations documented, with the employee's responses
- [ ] Support already given is listed
- [ ] Workload, leave, training and adjustment factors reviewed (adjustments → hr-employee-relations)
- [ ] Diagnosis recorded: skill / will / mixed — and why not structural
- [ ] HR has reviewed the draft before the employee sees it

## 2. Plan

```markdown
# Performance improvement plan — Employee A

- Role: [title]   Manager: [role]   HR partner: [role]
- Jurisdiction: [country, state/province]
- Plan period: [start] → [end]  ([N] weeks — per policy; [VERIFY: <country> law])
- Purpose: to set out where performance is below the role's expectations, what improvement is
  required, the support the company will provide, and how progress will be reviewed.

## Performance gaps (observable, dated, job-related)
1. [Area — e.g. deadline management]
   - Expectation: [what the role requires, where it was communicated and when]
   - Observed: [dated examples — "the [deliverable] due [date] was submitted [date]"]
   - Impact: [effect on team, customer, schedule]
2. [Area]
   - Expectation:
   - Observed:
   - Impact:

## Required improvement (measurable)
| # | Gap | Required result | How it is measured | By |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [deadline management] | [weekly update submitted by agreed day each week] | [tracker timestamp] | [date] |
| 2 | [work quality] | [deliverables include owners, dates, risks before review] | [manager review against checklist] | [date] |
| 3 | [escalation] | [blockers raised at least [N] business days before a deadline at risk] | [message / meeting note] | [date] |

## Support provided
- Manager: [priority review every [day] for the first [N] weeks]
- Resources: [template, tool access, documentation standard]
- Learning: [session / pairing / course] (→ hr-learning)
- Other: [workload adjustment, clarified priorities]

## Check-ins
- Cadence: [weekly], [duration], written notes shared with Employee A within [N] working days
- Mid-point review: [date]   Final review: [date]

## Outcome options (stated as policy, not threat)
- Requirements met → plan closes; normal feedback continues.
- Partly met with clear progress → one documented extension of [N] weeks with reasons.
- Not met → further action under [policy name], which may include [sanctions per policy],
  decided by people after review; *review with qualified employment counsel (or the relevant authority) before acting.*

## Employee response
Employee A may add written comments, which are kept with this plan.

Acknowledgement of receipt (not agreement): [signed in HRIS]
```

## 3. Initial meeting script (manager, HR present)

```text
I want to talk about a formal performance improvement plan. The aim is to be clear about where
things stand, what good looks like, and the support we'll provide — and to give you a fair,
defined opportunity to meet the role's expectations.

The concerns are [area 1], [area 2] and [area 3]. For each, here's what the role needs and what
we've seen, with examples. [Walk through section "Performance gaps".]

Here's what success looks like over the next [N] weeks and how we'll measure it. [Required improvement.]

Here's what I and the company will do to support you. [Support.] We'll meet [weekly] and I'll
share notes after each check-in.

What questions do you have? Is there anything affecting your work that we should know about?
You can also add written comments to the plan.
```

Delivery rules: private, scheduled, enough time; no surprises (concerns were raised before); stick to
evidence; do not speculate about motives or personal life; if the employee raises health, caring,
discrimination or harassment concerns, pause and route to HR / hr-employee-relations.

Record what happened, not adjectives. "Update submitted [day], complete against checklist" — not
"good effort".

## 5. Final review

- [ ] Each requirement assessed against its measure, with evidence
- [ ] Support actually delivered as promised (if not, the plan is not a fair test — extend or restart)
- [ ] Recommendation written by the manager, reviewed by HR
- [ ] If any sanction or termination is proposed: process handed to hr-employee-relations;
      *review with qualified employment counsel (or the relevant authority) before acting.* (hr-rules § 2)
- [ ] Outcome communicated in a meeting, then in writing
- [ ] PIP outcomes across the population reviewed in aggregate for group disparities (hr-rules § 5)

## Language guide

| Avoid | Use |
|---|---|
| "Lazy", "careless", "bad attitude" | "Submitted [deliverable] on [date], [N] days after the agreed date" |
| "You never..." / "always..." | "On [dates] ..." |
| "This is your last chance" | "If the requirements are not met, the next step under [policy] is ..." |
| "We'll see how it goes" | "Success means [measure] by [date]" |
| Any reference to age, health, family, leave | Nothing — these are not performance evidence |
