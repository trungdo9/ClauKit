## Announcement skeleton

```text
Subject: <what is changing> — <effective date>
What this means for you: <1–2 sentences, reader's perspective first>
What is changing: <specifics>
What is not changing: <pay, role, location, benefits — whichever applies>
Why: <honest reason, one paragraph>
What you need to do: <action + deadline, or "nothing">
Questions: <manager / channel / session date>
```

## Manager talking points

```markdown
- Core message (say it in your own words): <...>
- What is changing / not changing: <...>
- Hard questions and answers:
  - "Will there be job losses?" → <approved answer; never improvise>
  - "Who do I report to now?" → <...>
  - "Why weren't we told earlier?" → <...>
- If you don't know: "I'll find out and come back by <date>." Log the question to <channel>.
- Do not: speculate, share names, comment before the embargo.
```

## FAQ structure

Group by: what changes · timing · my role and pay · process and support · where to ask. Each answer ≤3 sentences; date-stamp the FAQ and record updates.

## Wellbeing crisis protocol skeleton

```markdown
1. Immediate danger to life → emergency services first; then notify <HR role>.
2. Disclosure to a manager → listen, do not diagnose or counsel; offer EAP/<occupational health>; agree immediate workload relief; inform HR with consent unless safety requires otherwise.
3. HR → confirm support offered, confidentiality limits explained, record minimal facts in the HRIS (not in plans/hr/).
4. Team impact → manager briefed on what can be shared (usually nothing personal); support offered to the team.
5. After-care → check-in schedule; return-to-work via occupational-health advice and hr-employee-relations for adjustments.
Contacts: EAP <number>, emergency <local number `[VERIFY]`>, HR on-call <role>.
Rehearsal date: <date> · Owner: <role>
```
