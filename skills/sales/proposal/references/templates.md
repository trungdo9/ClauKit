# Proposal — Templates

Templates for [proposal](../SKILL.md). Every proof point is cited or `[NEEDS DATA]`; projections are labelled with their assumptions.

## Win theme matrix

```markdown
# Win Theme Matrix: [opportunity-slug]

## Theme 1: [client-centric statement]
- Buyer need: [specific challenge from RFP or discovery — cite section/notes]
- Our differentiator: [capability, methodology, or asset]
- Proof point: [metric + source / case study / evidence]
- Appears in: [Executive Summary, Technical Approach §x, Case Study B, Pricing Rationale]
- Stress test: specific ☐ provable ☐ differentiating ☐ hard for a competitor to claim ☐

## Theme 2: [...]
## Theme 3: [...]

## Competitive positioning
| Evaluation factor | Our approach | Expected competitor approach | Why ours matters more to this buyer |
|-------------------|--------------|------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
```

## Executive summary

```markdown
# Executive Summary

[Buyer] faces [specific challenge, in their language]. [1–2 sentences showing understanding of situation, constraints, stakes.]

[Central tension: cost of inaction or opportunity at risk — quantified only with a sourced figure.]

[Thesis: 2–3 sentences — our approach and how it resolves the tension. Win themes surface here.]

[Proof: one concrete, cited evidence point.]

[Transformed state: what their organisation looks like [period] after implementation — specific, tied to their stated goals; projections labelled.]
```
