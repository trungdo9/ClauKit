# The TRIBE framework — run a Facebook Group as the organic surface

Groups are the 10–30× Page-reach goldmine — but only when run as a **community, not a broadcast**. TRIBE
structures the play. The agent designs the strategy and drafts the posts; **a human creates the Group,
posts inside it, approves members, and moderates.**

## T — Treat it as a community asset, not a broadcast

- A niche, **keyword-rich** name; **Private (Visible)** so it is findable in search but members-only
  inside; **2–3 membership questions**; clear rules on the tin.
- One specific audience. "Everyone" is not an audience, and a Group named for everyone reaches no one.

## R — Run two-way engagement daily

- Daily varied posts, **polls**, **short-form video**. Respond inside the **first 30–60 minutes**.
- **Never post and ghost.** An unanswered thread costs more reach than a skipped day.
- The cadence that makes this survivable → [[engagement-routine]].

## I — Invite quality, not blasts

- The email list and social followers (target ~30% acceptance, ~40% 30-day retention), **non-competing
  admin partnerships**, a **lead magnet**, **ads to the join link**, and **genuine** cross-posting in
  related Groups.
- **200–500 members per month.** No bulk-poster automation, no bought members — ToS violation, ban risk,
  and it wrecks the engagement ratio on the way down.

## B — Build trust before selling

- **Value first, trust second, sales third.** **No selling in the first 30 days.**
- A warm niche Group converts far better than cold traffic — but the community is the product, and
  revenue follows it. Never quote a revenue figure as a projection.

## E — Extend from the Page, keep the Group native

- The **Page and cross-channel content drive joins** — that is the funnel, and it is what a scheduler can
  actually publish → [[facebook-strategy]].
- **In-Group posting, admin, membership questions, moderation, and Group Insights are human and native.**
  ClauKit has no Group API.

## The Group plan a request should fill

```
ASSET:     niche + keyword-rich name + Private (Visible) + 2-3 membership questions + rules
GOAL:      community | leads | paid membership -- pick ONE; it changes every other line
ENGAGE:    daily varied posts + polls + short video; respond in the first 30-60 min;
           no selling for 30 days; no ghosting
GROW:      email/social invites + admin partnerships + lead magnet + ads to the join link
           + genuine cross-posting. 200-500/month. No automation, no bought members.
MODERATE:  rules, escalation path, named admin, consent log for reposted member content
MONETIZE:  value -> trust -> offers. Never fabricate revenue.
PUBLISH:   the Page + cross-channel posts that drive joins are schedulable.
           In-Group posting, admin, and moderation are human -> engagement-routine.
MEASURE:   native Group Insights, pulled by a human. Baseline BEFORE the first post.
```
