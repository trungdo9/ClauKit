# Research, types, and branded tags

How to choose tags that do their modest job well, and how to design branded and campaign hashtags — the one
place hashtags still pull real weight.

## The eight hashtag types

Mix types deliberately; each does a different job.

- **Topic** — what the post is about (`#ProductManagement`). Core categorization.
- **Niche / community** — a community you actually belong to (`#BuildInPublic`, `#BookTok`). Where engaged
  people browse and search.
- **Branded** — unique to the brand. Community and UGC. See below.
- **Campaign** — tied to a launch or event (`#[Brand]LaunchWeek`). Tracking and participation.
- **Product / service** — what you offer (`#SocialMediaScheduling`). Intent and search.
- **Location** — geographic, for local discovery (`#RotterdamStartups`). Powerful for local businesses.
- **Format** — when the format is the value (`#Tutorial`, `#CaseStudy`). Use sparingly.
- **Event / seasonal** — a live moment (`#SaaStrAnnual`). Time-bound only.

A typical post uses a few across **topic plus niche plus sometimes branded or location** — never all eight.

## Research and vetting

Choosing relevant, mid-tier, safe tags matters far more than the count.

1. **Relevance first.** The tag must match the actual content. Irrelevant trending tags confuse the
   algorithm and can cost reach.
2. **Aim mid-tier (~10K–500K posts).** Mega-tags with millions of posts bury you in seconds; ultra-tiny tags
   have no audience. Mid-tier is real demand you can compete in.
3. **Open the tag page before using it.** Are the top posts like yours? Is there genuine engagement? Is it
   overrun by bots and spam? If it looks spammy or off-topic, skip it.
4. **Avoid banned or flagged tags.** Some innocuous-looking tags are spam-associated and suppress reach. If
   a tag's page looks blocked or sketchy, do not use it.
5. **Find them natively.** Use the platform's own search and suggestions, and look at what well-performing
   creators **in the same niche** actually use. Analyze their approach; do not copy a list wholesale.

## Rotating sets, not one pasted block

- Build **2–4 reusable sets per pillar or topic**, then rotate by what each post is actually about.
- Rotation keeps tags **relevant per post**, which is the thing that matters, and avoids the low-effort
  copy-paste pattern.
- Keep **1–2 consistent branded tags** across posts; vary the rest.

## Branded and campaign hashtags — the durable use

This is where hashtags still earn real value: community, UGC, and tracking.

**Branded hashtag** (ongoing): a short, unique, memorable tag for the brand or community that followers use
when they post about it. Aggregates user-generated content and builds identity.

**Campaign hashtag** (time-bound): tied to a specific launch, event, or promotion, for participation and
tracking.

Design rules:

- **Unique and unused.** Search it first and confirm it is not already taken or attached to something
  off-brand.
- **Short, easy to spell, hard to typo.** Read it aloud. Check for accidental other words when the letters
  run together.
- **On-brand** and consistent across the whole campaign.
- **Promote it** — in the bio, in captions, in the CTA ("share yours with #___") — or nobody adopts it.
- **Track it** through the platform's native search.

## Honest measurement

There is **no hashtag analytics surface** in ClauKit. To see what is working, the user pulls **native
insights** — for example Instagram's per-post "reach from hashtags" — or reviews qualitatively.

Treat hashtag choices as **experiments to observe**, not optimizations this skill can prove. **Never claim
performance data nobody has pulled.** Reporting and attribution → [[analytics]].
