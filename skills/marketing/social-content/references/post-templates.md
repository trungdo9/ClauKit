# Post Format Templates

Fill-in shapes for the highest-frequency post types, plus the hook library. Everything here is a starting structure — the brand voice in `plans/marketing-context.md` overrides any phrasing below, and forbidden-word lists apply to hooks first.

## Contents
- LinkedIn (Story, Contrarian Take, List, How-To)
- Twitter/X threads (Tutorial, Story, Breakdown)
- Instagram (Carousel, Reel)
- Hook library (Curiosity, Story, Value, Contrarian, Social Proof)

## LinkedIn

### The Story Post
```
[Hook: unexpected outcome or lesson]

[Set the scene: when/where this happened]

[The challenge you faced]

[What you tried / what happened]

[The turning point]

[The result]

[The lesson for the reader]

[Question to prompt engagement]
```

### The Contrarian Take
```
[Unpopular opinion stated flatly]

Here's why:

[Reason 1]
[Reason 2]
[Reason 3]

[What you recommend instead]

[Invite discussion: "Am I wrong?"]
```

### The List Post
```
[X things I learned about [topic] after [credibility builder]:]

1. [Point] — [brief explanation]

2. [Point] — [brief explanation]

3. [Point] — [brief explanation]

[Wrap-up insight]

Which resonates most?
```

### The How-To
```
How to [outcome] in [timeframe]:

Step 1: [Action]
  - [Why this matters]

Step 2: [Action]
  - [Key detail]

Step 3: [Action]
  - [Common mistake to avoid]

[Result you can expect]

[CTA or question]
```

## Twitter/X Threads

### The Tutorial Thread
```
Post 1: [Hook + explicit promise]
        "Here's exactly how to [outcome], step by step:"

Post 2-7: [One step each, with the detail that makes it real]

Final:  [Recap + CTA]
        "If this helped, follow for more on [topic]."
```

### The Story Thread
```
Post 1: [Intriguing hook]
        "[Time] ago, [unexpected thing happened]. The full story:"

Post 2-6: [Story beats, tension rising]

Post 7: [Resolution and the lesson]

Final:  [Takeaway + engagement ask]
```

### The Breakdown Thread
```
Post 1: [Company/person] just [did thing].
        Here's why it works (and what to steal):

Post 2-6: [Analysis points, one per post]

Post 7: [Your key takeaway]

Final:  [Related insight + follow CTA]
```

**Thread rules:** the hook post must stand alone and earn the next tap. Never bury the payoff past post 3 without paying something out first. One idea per post; if a post needs two, split it.

## Instagram

### Carousel (generic shape)
```
Slide 1:      [Bold statement or question — this is the feed thumbnail]
Slides 2-9:   [One point per slide, visual + text]
Slide 10:     [Summary + single CTA]

Caption:      [Expand the topic, add context, restate the hook, one CTA]
```

For five full slide-by-slide narrative architectures with per-slide copy slots, see [carousel-frameworks.md](carousel-frameworks.md). Pick the framework before writing slides.

### Reel Script (short form)
```
Hook  (0-2s):   [Pattern interrupt or bold claim]
Setup (2-5s):   [Context for the tip]
Value (5-25s):  [The actual advice]
CTA   (25-30s): [Follow, comment, share, link in bio]
```

Fuller scripting template and hook library in [short-form-video.md](short-form-video.md).

## Hook Library

The first line decides whether anything after it gets read. Draft five, keep one.

### Curiosity
- "I was wrong about [common belief]."
- "The real reason [outcome] happens isn't what you think."
- "[Result] — and it only took [surprisingly short time]."
- "Nobody talks about [insider knowledge]."

### Story
- "Last week, [unexpected thing] happened."
- "I almost [big mistake]."
- "3 years ago, I [past state]. Today, [current state]."
- "[Person] told me something I'll never forget."

### Value
- "How to [outcome] (without [common pain]):"
- "[Number] [things] that [outcome]:"
- "The simplest way to [outcome]:"
- "Stop [common mistake]. Do this instead:"

### Contrarian
- "Unpopular opinion: [bold statement]"
- "[Common advice] is wrong. Here's why:"
- "I stopped [common practice] and [result]."
- "Everyone says [X]. The truth is [Y]."

### Social Proof
- "We [result] in [timeframe]. Here's the story:"
- "[Number] people asked me about [topic]. Here's my answer:"
- "[Authority figure] taught me [lesson]."

**Grounding rule:** any bracket that holds a number, a result, or a claim must be filled from something real — a measured outcome, a shipped feature, a documented customer story. An unfilled or invented number makes the hook a fabrication, and social is the surface where that gets audited publicly.
