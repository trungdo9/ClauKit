# Platform Strategy Guide

Per-platform execution notes for organic posting. Format, cadence, and algorithm behaviour — not channel selection (that is [[content-strategy]]) and not paid placements (that is [[ad-creative]]).

**Version-sensitive.** Everything below about algorithm weighting, reach penalties, and "best times" reflects the upstream source's observations, not a standing guarantee. Platforms change ranking behaviour without notice — treat these as starting hypotheses to test against the account's own analytics, and verify before relying on any single claim.

## LinkedIn

**Best for:** B2B, thought leadership, professional networking, recruiting
**Audience:** Professionals, decision-makers, job seekers
**Posting frequency:** 3-5x per week
**Best times (test, don't assume):** Tuesday-Thursday, 7-8am, 12pm, 5-6pm

**What works:**
- Personal stories with business lessons
- Contrarian takes on industry topics
- Behind-the-scenes of building a company
- Data and original insights
- Carousel posts (document format)
- Polls that spark discussion

**What doesn't:**
- Overly promotional content
- Generic motivational quotes
- Links in the main post (suppresses reach)
- Corporate speak without personality

**Format:**
- First line is everything — it is the only thing visible before "see more"
- Line breaks for readability; one idea per line block
- 1,200-1,500 characters performs well
- Links in the first comment, not the post body
- Tag people sparingly and only when they will genuinely respond

**Algorithm behaviour (verify):**
- First-hour engagement weighs heaviest
- Comments > reactions > clicks
- Dwell time (people actually reading) signals quality
- Document posts (carousels) get strong reach
- Polls drive engagement volume but do not build authority

## Twitter/X

**Best for:** Tech, media, real-time commentary, community building
**Audience:** Tech-savvy, news-oriented, niche communities
**Posting frequency:** 3-10x per day including replies
**Best times:** Varies heavily by audience — measure, don't copy

**What works:**
- Hot takes and opinions
- Threads that teach something concrete
- Behind-the-scenes moments
- Replying inside other people's conversations
- Memes and humour when genuinely on-brand
- Real-time commentary on events

**What doesn't:**
- Pure self-promotion
- Threads without a strong first-tweet hook
- Ignoring replies and mentions
- Scheduling everything (no real-time presence)

**Format:**
- Short standalone tweets (under ~100 chars) tend to outperform long ones
- Threads: hook in tweet 1, promise value, deliver, recap, CTA
- Quote-posts with added insight beat plain reposts
- Visuals stop the scroll

**Algorithm behaviour (verify):**
- Replies and quote-posts build authority faster than original posts
- Threads keep people on platform and are rewarded for it
- Images and video get more reach than text-only
- Engagement in the first ~30 minutes matters most
- Paid tiers may boost reach — treat as unstable

## Instagram

**Best for:** Visual brands, lifestyle, e-commerce, younger demographics
**Audience:** 18-44, visual-first consumers
**Posting frequency:** 1-2 feed posts per day, 3-10 Stories per day
**Best times (test):** 11am-1pm, 7-9pm

**What works:**
- High-quality visuals
- Behind-the-scenes Stories
- Reels (short-form video)
- Carousels that carry real value
- User-generated content
- Interactive Stories (polls, questions)

**What doesn't:**
- Low-quality images
- Walls of text baked into images
- Ignoring Stories and Reels
- Only promotional content

**Format:**
- Reels reach far past static posts — the upstream source cites roughly 2x; treat the direction as reliable, the multiple as not
- The first frame of a Reel is the hook
- Carousels: up to 10 slides of educational content (see [carousel-frameworks.md](carousel-frameworks.md))
- Caption carries the searchable text — front-load before the ~125-char truncation

**Algorithm behaviour (verify):**
- Reels prioritized over static posts
- Saves and shares outweigh likes
- Stories keep the account top-of-feed
- Consistency beats polish

## TikTok

**Best for:** Brand awareness, younger audiences, viral reach
**Audience:** 16-34, entertainment-focused
**Posting frequency:** 1-4x per day
**Best times (test):** 7-9am, 12-3pm, 7-11pm

**What works:**
- Native, unpolished content
- Trending sounds and formats
- Educational content in an entertaining wrapper
- POV and day-in-the-life content
- Replying to comments with videos
- Duets and stitches

**What doesn't:**
- Overly produced content
- Ignoring trends entirely
- Hard selling
- Repurposed horizontal video

**Format:**
- Hook in the first 1-2 seconds
- Start under 30 seconds
- Vertical only (9:16)
- Trending sounds while the trend is still rising
- Consistency trains the algorithm on who to show you to

---

## Facebook

**Best for:** Communities, local businesses, older demographics, Groups
**Audience:** 25-55+, community-oriented
**Posting frequency:** 1-2x per day
**Best times (test):** 1-4pm weekdays

**What works:**
- Facebook Groups (community)
- Native video
- Live video
- Local content and events
- Questions that prompt discussion

**What doesn't:**
- Links to external sites (reach penalty)
- Pure promotional content
- Ignoring comments
- Cross-posting from other platforms without adaptation
