# Product Hunt Launch Playbook

Product Hunt can be powerful for reaching early adopters — but it is not magic, and it rewards preparation done weeks in advance.

## Trade-offs

**Pros**
- Exposure to a tech-savvy early-adopter audience.
- Credibility bump, especially with Product of the Day.
- Potential PR coverage and backlinks.

**Cons**
- Very competitive to rank well.
- Traffic spike is short-lived.
- Requires significant pre-launch planning.

## Before launch day

1. Build relationships with influential supporters, content hubs, and communities — weeks ahead, not the night before.
2. Optimize the listing: compelling tagline, polished visuals, short demo video.
3. Study successful launches in your category and identify what actually worked.
4. Engage in relevant communities — provide value before pitching anything.
5. Prepare the team for all-day engagement.

## On launch day

1. Treat it as an all-day event, not a post.
2. Respond to every comment in real time.
3. Answer questions and spark discussion rather than thanking people.
4. Encourage your existing audience to engage.
5. Direct traffic back to your own site to capture signups.

## After launch day

1. Follow up with everyone who engaged.
2. Convert the traffic into owned relationships — email signups above all.
3. Keep momentum with post-launch content instead of letting the spike decay.

## Case studies

**SavvyCal** (scheduling tool)
- Optimized landing page and onboarding *before* launching.
- Built relationships with productivity/SaaS influencers in advance.
- Responded to every comment on launch day.
- Result: #2 Product of the Month.

**Reform** (form builder)
- Studied successful launches and applied the insights.
- Crafted a clear tagline, polished visuals, and a demo video.
- Engaged in communities beforehand, providing value first.
- Treated the launch as an all-day engagement event.
- Directed traffic to capture signups.
- Result: #1 Product of the Day.

## Adjacent platforms

The same shape applies to BetaList, Hacker News, and category marketplaces: a listing worth clicking, a pre-warmed audience, live presence for the whole day, and a path from the platform into an owned channel. Only the ranking mechanics differ.
