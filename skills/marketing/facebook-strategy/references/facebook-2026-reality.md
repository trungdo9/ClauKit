## The shift: an AI discovery engine, not a Page-broadcast feed

Page **organic reach has collapsed** to roughly **1.65%** of followers (reported range **1.6–5.9%**) —
but this is not Facebook declining. The algorithm shifted to proactive AI recommendation: up to
**35–50% of feed views are "unconnected" content** from accounts the user does not follow.

It ranks in four steps — **Inventory → Signals → Predictions → Relevance Score** — and uses **NLP to read
captions and video transcripts**. Facebook now behaves like a **search and discovery engine**.
Distribution depends on how well the AI understands the content, not on how many people follow the Page.

## The signals that matter

- **Saves and shares outrank likes and reactions.** Meaningful comments, long-form replies, shares to
  Stories, dwell time, and watch time rank highest.
- **Engagement velocity in the first 30–60 minutes is decisive.** Roughly 10 genuine comments in 30
  minutes is reported to reach 3–5× more people than the same comments spread over 24 hours. Reply early.
- **Thematic consistency.** The algorithm reads the **last 9–12 posts** to tag an account. Scattered
  topics lower reach. **Focus ~2 core themes**; the occasional off-topic post will not derail it.

## Surfaces and formats

- **All videos are Reels** (since mid-2025). **Reels carry the highest reach and discovery** — pushed to
  non-followers **by content category, not by brand**. Page Reels commonly out-reach Page posts several-
  fold. The first 3 seconds, retention, and completion decide distribution.
- **Photos draw the highest feed engagement**; **carousels** carry storytelling. Video is shared far more
  than other formats. **Longer 3–8 minute video** is now also boosted, on "time spent".
- **Links perform worst** — they leak users off-platform. Put the **link in the first comment**, or use a
  native Shop.

## Groups are the reach goldmine

**1.8B monthly Group users.** Groups reach roughly **20–40% of members** versus **1–6% for Pages** — a
post in a 10k-member Group reaches on the order of 2,000–4,000 people versus 200–600 for a same-size
Page. Group posts also rank higher in Feed, on the community signal.

The 2026 experience is Groups-first: Meta algorithmically favors Groups, which keep people on-platform,
while monetizing Pages through ads. Owning a Group captures materially more organic reach. The full play
is in [[facebook-groups]].

## Honest fit and caveats

- **Small, specialized Pages can out-reach large ones.** Niche relevance beats scale.
- **AI-generated content is treated the same as human content** — no penalty — **but** low-quality AI-spam
  detection is rising, and there is a real **authenticity premium**: behind-the-scenes, personal stories,
  and founder-led content outperform.
- **3B+ users, older-skewing.** Strong for community, local, B2C, and discovery. Weak for pure Page-feed
  broadcasting.
- **Engagement bait** ("like & share") is penalized.
- **Meta retains roughly 90 days of insight data.** Snapshot on a schedule, or the baseline is gone.

## The strategic pattern

**Reels for unconnected discovery → Feed for connected depth and trust → Groups for community.** Reels put
the brand in front of new people; Feed gives them a reason to stay; Groups turn them into a community.
