# Surfaces, recipes, and two worked examples

## The three-surface system (play each for its job)

| Surface | Job | Optimize for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reels** | unconnected **discovery** | first 3s, retention, completion, shares | pushed by category, not brand; out-reaches Page posts several-fold |
| **Feed** | connected **depth and trust** | comments, saves, shares, dwell | photos (engagement), carousels, 3–8 min video |
| **Groups** | **community** | meaningful discussion, member reach | 20–40% member reach; human-run → [[facebook-groups]] |

**The pairing:** for each theme, ship a **Reel (discovery)** and a **Feed post (depth)**. Discovery turns
into relationship. Do not publish "for everyone" — one viewer, ~2 themes.

## Per-surface recipes

- **Reel (discovery):** hook in the first 3 seconds → 1–3 points → payoff. Vertical, captioned, native to
  Facebook (a competitor's watermark or the wrong aspect ratio suppresses reach). Category keywords in the
  caption. Script craft → [[social-content]].
- **Feed photo or carousel (depth):** a value post — a mini-tutorial, a before/after, or a real opinion that
  invites thoughtful replies. Any **link goes in the first comment**. Ask a genuine question.
- **Long video (authority):** a 3–8 minute explainer, demo, or breakdown, for time-spent and trust.
- **Group post (community):** ask something, share a win, start a discussion — as a member, not a billboard.
  Human-posted, replies handled live.

## Link and format rules

- **Link in the first comment**, never the post body. Links are the lowest-reach format.
- Every outbound link carries **UTM parameters**. A post whose traffic cannot be attributed did not happen.
- **No engagement bait** ("like & share"). **Do not** mass-post identical content to many Groups — that is
  spam, and it costs the account.
- **Saves and shares beat likes.** Make the post worth saving and worth sending to a friend.

## Worked example 1 — Reel + Feed pairing (blunt, practical voice)

```
THEME: freelancer tax (one of 2 core themes)
REEL (discovery): "Most freelancers overpay tax. One fix." -> 3 fast points -> "do this today."
                  Vertical, captioned, keyword in the caption.
FEED (depth):     Carousel "The 30% rule, step by step" + a real question: "What's your setup?"
                  Link to the guide in the FIRST COMMENT, with UTM.
ENGAGE:           Publish 09:00. A named human replies to every comment in the first hour.
MEASURE:          Native Insights: reach, saves, shares per surface. Snapshot Friday.
```

## Worked example 2 — Group community + Feed (warm, service voice)

```
THEME: calm bookkeeping
GROUP (human):    In a small-business Group: "What's the one money task you keep avoiding? No judgment."
                  Reply to every answer. Posted natively by a person.
FEED (depth):     Soft photo + "The one rule we give every client" mini-tutorial; a question that invites replies.
REEL (discovery): 20s founder-to-camera, "the panic, fixed." Newsletter link in the first comment, with UTM.
MEASURE:          Native Insights + Group Insights (acceptance, engaged members). Nothing invented.
```

Both examples: read `plans/marketing-context.md` first; ~2 themes; a Reel for discovery, a Feed post for
depth, a Group post for community; links in the first comment with UTM; no bait; first-hour replies by a
named human. Reel and caption craft → [[social-content]]; the reply cadence → [[engagement-routine]];
posting itself is manual — ClauKit does not publish to Facebook.
