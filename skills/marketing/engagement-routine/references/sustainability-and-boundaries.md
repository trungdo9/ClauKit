# Sustainability and boundaries

The half of "engagement routine" that most advice skips, and the reason routines fail. Engagement is the
activity most likely to bleed into every waking hour, and burnout in the field is well documented — a
large share of social professionals report falling motivation and creativity, and roughly **46%** report
having considered leaving the work over stress.

A routine that is not sustainable does not get followed. **Sustainability is a performance requirement,
not a soft extra.**

## The core boundaries

- **Notification discipline.** Do not check constantly. **Turn off non-essential notifications outside the
  windows.** Engagement happens in scheduled blocks and the golden hour — not in a 14-hour ambient drip.
- **State the response window, and communicate it.** "We reply within a day" is a perfectly good promise.
  Decide a realistic response time and let it be acceptable not to be instant on everything. Genuine
  crises are the exception, and they need a named escalation path.
- **Protect off-hours.** Evenings and weekends are not for clearing comments. The scheduled queue keeps the
  brand present (→ `the-scheduling-engagement-flywheel.md`) so nobody has to be "on" to stay visible.
- **Small repeatable systems over heroics.** A modest routine that actually gets kept beats an ambitious
  one abandoned in a week. Templates, recurring windows, and a short checklist cut the daily decision load.

## Consistency, sustainably

Showing up consistently matters — the penalty for going quiet is real. But **consistency means a pace that
can be held for months, not a sprint that ends in a crash.** Pick the smallest routine that is genuinely
consistent, even 2×10 minutes a day; protect the golden hour; and grow it only while it stays holdable.

## Scale to capacity, honestly

- **Busy week?** Shrink the windows, keep the golden hour, and let proactive commenting drop first. Do not
  abandon the routine — downsize it.
- **More capacity or more platforms?** Add a window or a platform, but **concentrate where the audience
  actually is** rather than thinning across all of them.
- **A team?** Define who covers which window and what gets escalated. Same triage, shared. Every window
  gets a named owner.

## Genuine only — the line that protects the account

Under time pressure the temptation is to **automate or fake** engagement: bots, mass auto-comments, bought
interactions, pods. Do not. It is detected, penalized, and self-defeating, and it is the fast road to a
damaged account.

The sustainable answer is a **smaller, genuine routine**, with the agent **drafting** to save time and a
**human** doing the real interacting. Less, real, and consistent wins.
