# The daily and weekly routine

The actual cadence. Engagement is the highest-return activity in social — replying lifts engagement on
every platform — but only when it is a **habit, not a heroic burst**. This is the operating system that
turns reply craft into a repeatable routine.

> Re-verify timing specifics quarterly. The structure — windows plus a golden hour, batched rather than
> all-day — is durable.

## The principle: windows, not all-day

Do not graze notifications all day. It wrecks focus and burns people out. **Batch engagement into a few
dedicated windows.** A common, sustainable shape:

- **~20 minutes in the morning** and **~20 minutes in the afternoon** — adjust to real capacity.
- Plus a **golden-hour pass** right after each post publishes.

## The golden hour (non-negotiable)

The **first ~15–20 minutes** after a post goes live is the highest-leverage engagement window. Early
replies build the comment thread and signal the algorithm, which lifts reach.

**Publish when someone can be present**, and spend that window replying to early comments. This is
precisely why posts get scheduled for times a human can show up — see
`the-scheduling-engagement-flywheel.md`.

## Order of operations (each window)

Spend the time in priority order. Full triage in `triage-priorities-and-goals.md`.

1. **Reply to comments** on recent posts — golden hour first.
2. **Respond to DMs** — relationships and conversions live here.
3. **Handle anything negative or urgent** quickly.
4. **Proactive commenting** — add real value on 3–5 posts from **ICP, peers, and niche leaders**. This
   borrows their audience.

The agent **drafts** these in brand voice ([[social-content]], [[copywriting]]); a **human reviews and
posts** them in-app.

## A worked daily template (solo brand, ~40 min/day)

- **Morning (20 min):** reply to overnight comments (~5) → reply to DMs (~3) → 3 proactive comments on
  ICP or leader posts.
- **At publish time (golden hour, ~15 min):** reply to every early comment on the new post; ask a
  question back to keep threads alive.
- **Afternoon (20 min):** clear new comments and DMs → 2–3 more proactive comments → note any recurring
  question worth turning into content.

## Weekly rhythm

- **Once a week (~30 min):** find 10–15 accounts or threads worth engaging with (ICP, peers, leaders) for
  the coming week; collect the recurring questions worth converting into posts (→ [[content-strategy]]);
  skim what landed, natively, and do more of it.
- **Snapshot the native insights** the same day each week. Meta retains roughly 90 days; an un-snapshotted
  baseline is gone permanently.
- **Protect the boundaries** — `sustainability-and-boundaries.md`.

## Scale the routine to reality

- **Time:** an hour a day is a healthy ceiling for most solo brands. 40 minutes works. Even 2×10 minutes
  beats sporadic bursts. **Consistency beats intensity.**
- **Platforms:** more platforms means more time. **Concentrate where the audience actually is** rather
  than spreading thin across everything the brand publishes to.
- **Capacity:** when busy, shrink the windows but **keep the golden hour** — it is the highest return in
  the routine.
- **Owner:** every window has a named person. An unowned window is an unworked window.
