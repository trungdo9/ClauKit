## Content & SEO (1-10)

1. **Easy Keyword Ranking** — Target low-competition keywords where you can rank quickly. Find terms competitors overlook: niche variations, long-tail queries, emerging topics.
2. **SEO Audit** — Audit your own site technically and publish the findings. Documenting fixes builds authority while fixing the site.
3. **Glossary Marketing** — Comprehensive glossaries defining industry terms. Each term becomes a page targeting "what is X" searches.
4. **Programmatic SEO** — Template-driven pages at scale against a keyword pattern. Location pages, comparison pages, integration pages — any pattern with volume.
5. **Content Repurposing** — One piece becomes many formats: blog post → thread → video → podcast episode → infographic.
6. **Proprietary Data Content** — Original research from data only your product has. Competitors cannot replicate it, so it earns links.
7. **Internal Linking** — Strategic internal links distribute authority and improve crawlability. Build topical clusters.
8. **Content Refreshing** — Update existing content with fresh data and examples. Refreshed pages often outperform new ones.
9. **Knowledge Base SEO** — Optimize help docs for search. Support articles catch problem-solution queries from users already looking for a fix.
10. **Parasite SEO** — Publish on high-authority platforms (Medium, LinkedIn, Substack) that rank faster than your own domain.

## Competitor & Comparison (11-13)

11. **Competitor Comparison Pages** — "[Your Product] vs [Competitor]" pages capture high-intent searchers already comparing.
12. **Marketing Jiu-Jitsu** — Turn a competitor's weakness into your campaign. They raise prices, you run an affordability campaign.
13. **Competitive Ad Research** — Study competitor ads (Facebook Ad Library, SpyFu) to learn which messaging they keep paying for.

## Free Tools & Engineering (14-22)

14. **Side Projects as Marketing** — Small, useful tools adjacent to the main product; they attract users who later convert.
15. **Engineering as Marketing** — Free tools that solve a real problem — calculators, analyzers, generators — leading naturally to the paid product.
16. **Importers as Marketing** — "Import from [Competitor]" tools that remove switching friction.
17. **Quiz Marketing** — Interactive quizzes and assessments that engage while qualifying leads.
18. **Calculator Marketing** — ROI calculators, pricing estimators, savings tools. They attract links and rank well.
19. **Chrome Extensions** — Browser extensions with standalone value; the Web Store is another distribution channel.
20. **Microsites** — Focused sites for one campaign, product, or audience. Dedicated domains can rank faster.
21. **Scanners** — Free tools that audit or analyze something: site scanners, security checkers, performance analyzers.
22. **Public APIs** — An open API lets developers build on you, creating an ecosystem.

## Paid Advertising (23-34)

23. **Podcast Advertising** — Sponsor relevant shows. Host-read ads carry the host's credibility.
24. **Pre-targeting Ads** — Run awareness ads before direct-response campaigns; warm audiences convert better.
25. **Facebook Ads** — Detailed targeting plus retargeting; test creative variations.
26. **Instagram Ads** — Visual-first products; Stories and Reels placements.
27. **Twitter/X Ads** — Reach professionals already discussing the category.
28. **LinkedIn Ads** — Target by job title, company size, industry. Premium CPMs justified by B2B intent.
29. **Reddit Ads** — Passionate communities, but transparency is mandatory — ad-speak is punished.
30. **Quora Ads** — Users actively asking the question your product answers.
31. **Google Ads** — High-intent search: brand terms, competitor terms, category terms.
32. **YouTube Ads** — Pre-roll and discovery ads against related-content viewers.
33. **Cross-Platform Retargeting** — Follow users across platforms with one consistent message.
34. **Click-to-Messenger Ads** — Open a conversation instead of sending traffic to a landing page.

## Social Media & Community (35-44)

35. **Community Marketing** — Build and nurture a community: Slack, Discord, forum, or group.
36. **Quora Marketing** — Answer questions with genuine expertise; mention the product only where it fits.
37. **Reddit Keyword Research** — Mine Reddit for the exact language, pain points, and desires of the audience.
38. **Reddit Marketing** — Participate authentically in relevant subreddits. Value first, always.
39. **LinkedIn Audience** — Founder/personal brand for B2B reach; thought leadership builds authority.
40. **Instagram Audience** — Visual storytelling, behind-the-scenes, user stories.
41. **X Audience** — Consistent value in public; threads and insights compound into a following.
42. **Short Form Video** — TikTok, Reels, Shorts reach audiences no blog post will.
43. **Engagement Pods** — Coordinate with peers to boost each other's content.
44. **Comment Marketing** — Thoughtful comments on other people's content buy visibility with time instead of money.

## Email Marketing (45-53)

45. **Mistake Email Marketing** — The genuine "oops" email. Authenticity outperforms polish.
46. **Reactivation Emails** — Targeted campaigns at churned or inactive users.
47. **Founder Welcome Email** — A personal welcome from the founder creates a relationship the product cannot.
48. **Dynamic Email Capture** — Capture that adapts to behavior: exit intent, scroll depth, page context.
49. **Monthly Newsletters** — Consistency keeps the brand present between purchase moments.
50. **Inbox Placement** — Authentication and list hygiene. Deliverability is a technical problem, not a copy problem.
51. **Onboarding Emails** — Sequences that walk a new user to activation.
52. **Win-back Emails** — Give a churned user a concrete reason to return.
53. **Trial Reactivation** — Expired trials are a warm list, not a dead one.

## Partnerships & Programs (54-64)

54. **Affiliate Discovery Through Backlinks** — Find affiliates by analyzing who already links to competitors.
55. **Influencer Whitelisting** — Run your ads through an influencer's account for native credibility.
56. **Reseller Programs** — Let agencies resell or white-label; each becomes a distribution channel.
57. **Expert Networks** — Certified experts who implement your product for others.
58. **Newsletter Swaps** — Trade mentions with complementary newsletters.
59. **Article Quotes** — Supply expert quotes to journalists (HARO and successors).
60. **Pixel Sharing** — Share remarketing audiences with complementary companies.
61. **Shared Slack Channels** — Direct channels with partners and key customers.
62. **Affiliate Program** — Structured commissions for referrers.
63. **Integration Marketing** — Joint launches and co-marketing with integration partners.
64. **Community Sponsorship** — Sponsor the communities, newsletters, and publications your ICP already reads.

## Events & Speaking (65-72)

65. **Live Webinars** — Educational sessions that demonstrate expertise and generate leads.
66. **Virtual Summits** — Multi-speaker online events; the speakers bring their audiences.
67. **Roadshows** — Take the product on the road to meet customers in person.
68. **Local Meetups** — Host or attend meetups in key markets.
69. **Meetup Sponsorship** — Cheap access to an engaged local audience.
70. **Conference Speaking** — A stage borrows the conference's authority.
71. **Conferences** — Host your own and become the center of the category.
72. **Conference Sponsorship** — Buy visibility where the buyers already gather.

## PR & Media (73-76)

73. **Media Acquisitions as Marketing** — Acquire a newsletter, podcast, or publication in your space.
74. **Press Coverage** — Pitch a newsworthy story, not a product update.
75. **Fundraising PR** — Use the funding announcement as the news hook.
76. **Documentaries** — Documentary content about the industry or its customers.

## Launches & Promotions (77-86)

77. **Black Friday Promotions** — Annual deal moment; creates an acquisition spike.
78. **Product Hunt Launch** — Structured launch to reach early adopters.
79. **Early-Access Referrals** — Referrals buy earlier access during a launch or waitlist.
80. **New Year Promotions** — Fresh budgets and goal-setting energy.
81. **Early Access Pricing** — Discounted early tiers in exchange for going first.
82. **Product Hunt Alternatives** — BetaList, Launching Next, AlternativeTo, and similar.
83. **Twitter/X Giveaways** — Engagement giveaways gated on follow or repost.
84. **Giveaways** — Attention plus a captured lead list.
85. **Vacation Giveaways** — A grand prize large enough to travel on its own.
86. **Lifetime Deals** — One-time payment for cash and users now, at the cost of future MRR.

## Product-Led Growth (87-96)

87. **Powered By Marketing** — "Powered by [Product]" badges buy free impressions in customers' own surfaces.
88. **Free Migrations** — Do the switching work for them.
89. **Contract Buyouts** — Pay to end the competitor's contract early.
90. **One-Click Registration** — OAuth and minimal signup friction.
91. **In-App Upsells** — Upgrade prompts placed at the moment of need.
92. **Newsletter Referrals** — Built-in referral mechanics inside the newsletter.
93. **Viral Loops** — Product mechanics where usage creates exposure.
94. **Offboarding Flows** — A cancellation flow that either saves the user or extracts the reason.
95. **Concierge Setup** — White-glove onboarding for high-value accounts.
96. **Onboarding Optimization** — Continuous improvement of time-to-value.
