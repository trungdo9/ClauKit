# Copy Editing Checklist

Final QA pass before delivering edited copy. Runs alongside the Seven Sweeps in `../SKILL.md` — the sweeps do the work, this catches what the sweeps missed.

## Before starting

- [ ] `plans/marketing-context.md` loaded — brand voice, terminology, forbidden words, ICP
- [ ] Goal of the copy established (awareness / conversion / retention)
- [ ] Desired reader action identified
- [ ] Available proof/evidence inventoried with the author
- [ ] Read through once without editing

## Clarity (Sweep 1)

- [ ] Every sentence understandable on one read
- [ ] No jargon without explanation
- [ ] Pronouns have unambiguous referents
- [ ] No sentence trying to do too much
- [ ] One main idea per section; copy addresses the reader

## Voice and tone (Sweep 2)

- [ ] Formality level consistent throughout
- [ ] Brand personality maintained end to end
- [ ] No jarring mood shifts
- [ ] Forbidden words from `marketing-context.md` absent
- [ ] Reads well aloud

## So what (Sweep 3)

- [ ] Every feature connects to a benefit
- [ ] Every claim answers "why should I care?"
- [ ] Benefits map to real ICP desires
- [ ] No impressive-but-empty statements

## Prove it (Sweep 4)

- [ ] Claims substantiated
- [ ] Social proof specific and attributed
- [ ] Numbers and stats carry a source
- [ ] No unearned superlatives
- [ ] Nothing fabricated to fill a gap — unsourced claims either softened or marked `[NEEDS DATA]`

## Specificity (Sweep 5)

- [ ] Vague words replaced with concrete ones
- [ ] Numbers and timeframes present where they belong
- [ ] Generic statements made specific
- [ ] Filler that could not be made specific was cut, not decorated
- [ ] Every number traceable to context, author, or a source

## Heightened emotion (Sweep 6)

- [ ] Copy evokes feeling, not only information
- [ ] Pain points feel real
- [ ] Aspirations feel achievable
- [ ] Emotion authentic — no manufactured urgency or invented scarcity

## Zero risk (Sweep 7)

- [ ] Objections addressed near the CTA
- [ ] Trust signals present at the decision point
- [ ] Next step crystal clear
- [ ] Risk reversals stated (guarantee, trial, cancel-anytime)
- [ ] No hidden cost or surprise left implied

## Final checks

- [ ] No typos or grammatical errors
- [ ] Formatting consistent (headings, lists, capitalization)
- [ ] Links resolve
- [ ] Core message preserved through every edit
- [ ] Open `[NEEDS DATA]` flags listed for the author
- [ ] Output written per `.claude/workflows/marketing-rules.md` §6
